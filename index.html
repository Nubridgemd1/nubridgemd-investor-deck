01 The Problem

NPs can’t practice without a physician — and that relationship is a mess.

In most US states, nurse practitioners must have a collaborating / supervising physician to practice and prescribe. There is no marketplace for it — and every piece around it is manual and risky.

🧭

No marketplace

Finding a willing collaborating physician is slow and word-of-mouth.

📑

50 rulebooks

Each state has its own supervision rules, filings and ratios — easy and risky to get wrong.

🗂️

Manual credentialing

Payer credentialing (Medicare/Medicaid, CAQH, PECOS) is manual and opaque; agreements are papered ad-hoc.

461,000+ active-licensed NPs in the US (AANP, 2025) — one of the fastest-growing clinical professions. Every one needs a compliant physician relationship to practice.

02 The Solution & Product — live in production

One platform that runs the entire NP–physician relationship.

Not a prototype — full NP & physician onboarding, matching and messaging are live today at nubridgemd.com, with an in-house dual-party e-signature (no third-party provider) and NPPES registry automation.
